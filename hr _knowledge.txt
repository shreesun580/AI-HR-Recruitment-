AI HR Recruitment Knowledge Base

Python Developer:
Required skills: Python, SQL, Git, REST API, Machine Learning.
Important qualities: Problem solving, debugging and programming knowledge.

Data Analyst:
Required skills: Python, SQL, Excel, Power BI, Statistics.
Important qualities: Analytical thinking, data visualization and communication.

Web Developer:
Required skills: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React.
Important qualities: UI development, debugging and responsive design.

Software Engineer:
Required skills: Python, Java, SQL, Git, Data Structures, Algorithms.
Important qualities: Problem solving, coding and teamwork.

Recruitment Guidelines:
Candidates should be evaluated based on required skills,
relevant experience, projects and job requirements.

A candidate with 80% or higher skill match can be shortlisted.
A candidate with 60% to 79% match should be reviewed further.
A candidate below 60% may require additional training.
